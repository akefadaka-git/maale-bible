## Catalogue reconciliation

The Owner supplied a written response attributed to The Word for the World International, signed by Dr. Tesfaye Yohannes. It states that MDYTWF / mdyeth is the 2008 Full Bible, while DBL MDYBSE is a separate 2007 Maale New Testament published solely by the Bible Society of Ethiopia. The DBL RESERVED status belongs to that separate New Testament record and does not govern the 2008 Full Bible.

The response states that both rights holders authorized the 2008 Full Bible under CC BY-SA 4.0 and that the licence covers the eBible USFM package and supporting files. It states that no separate DBL agreement is required if licence conditions are met.

Detailed record: [Rights and Edition Reconciliation](../../docs/source-verification/RIGHTS_EDITION_RECONCILIATION_2026-11-07.md).

**Evidence qualification:** The project has the response text supplied by the Owner. Original email headers, sender address, delivery metadata and signatory authority have not been independently authenticated in this repository. Preserve the original email as primary evidence.

## Status vocabulary
