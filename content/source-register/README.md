# Maale Bible Source Register

This directory records candidate source metadata only. It must not contain scripture text or source archives during A2.

## Candidate record

A candidate mentioned during preliminary research is the eBible.org Maale entry, language identifier reported as mdy / mdyeth and edition identifier reported as MDYTWF. These identifiers and the associated license statement are **UNVERIFIED** for product use. They are leads for rights-holder reconciliation, not an approved source.

Candidate metadata URL: https://ebible.org/Bible/details.php?id=mdyeth

A separate Digital Bible Library record and separately distributed audio or New Testament-only materials have also been mentioned during preliminary research. Their relationship to the candidate full-Bible edition and their rights status must be independently reconciled. Do not combine editions or infer shared rights.

## Required fields for each candidate

- Stable candidate ID.
- Exact displayed title and language identifier.
- Edition/revision and testament/canon coverage.
- Publisher, copyright holder, translators and contributors.
- Original source URL and retrieval timestamp.
- Source package format and version.
- License text and version, if any.
- Evidence of licensing authority.
- Written product permission reference.
- Conflicting or reserved records.
- Reviewer, review date, decision and unresolved questions.

## Status vocabulary

- LEAD: discovered but not verified.
- UNDER_REVIEW: evidence collection in progress.
- RIGHTS_BLOCKED: permission missing or conflicting.
- APPROVED_FOR_INTAKE: Owner-approved written rights package exists.
- REJECTED: source is not selected.

No candidate may be marked APPROVED_FOR_INTAKE based only on this README or a web listing.
