## 2. Edition identity reconciled

The written response states:

- **MDYTWF / mdyeth** identifies the **2008 Maale Full Bible**, jointly held by The Word for the World International and the Bible Society of Ethiopia.
- **MDYBSE** identifies a distinct **2007 Maale New Testament**, published solely by the Bible Society of Ethiopia.
- The DBL RESERVED status belongs to the separate MDYBSE New Testament record and does not govern the 2008 MDYTWF full-Bible edition.

**Finding:** The two catalogue records are distinct works. The prior apparent conflict between the 2008 full Bible and the 2007 New Testament is reconciled by the written response.

## 3. Licence and package coverage

The response expressly states that both copyright holders authorised the exact 2008 full-Bible edition under **Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0)** and that the licence applies to the eBible USFM package and supporting files listed for MDYTWF / mdyeth, including the package identified as mdyeth_usfm.zip. The official eBible edition record independently lists MDYTWF / mdyeth as the 2008 Maale Full Bible, names both copyright holders, identifies CC BY-SA 4.0, and lists the USFM package (https://www.ebible.org/Scriptures/details.php?id=mdyeth).

It further states that no separate signed permission letter or DBL agreement is required to distribute that licensed full-Bible text through an independent Android application, provided the licence terms are followed.

**Finding:** On the face of the supplied rights-holder response, the licence and package-coverage questions are resolved. This is a documentary assessment, not an independent legal opinion or independent authentication of the email.

## 4. Permitted uses stated in the response

Subject to CC BY-SA 4.0 compliance, the response confirms permission for:

- Offline storage and local caching of scripture text.
- Display of the text in the application.
- Search indexing.
- Bookmarks and user annotations.
- User sharing of the text.

Any adaptations of the scripture text must be distributed under the same CC BY-SA 4.0 terms.

## 5. Mandatory notices and compliance controls

The response requires the application to include this attribution:

> Maale Bible (ጌኤዦ ማፃኣፖ) © 2008 The Word for the World International and the Bible Society of Ethiopia.

The application must also:

1. Link to https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/.
2. Indicate changes made, including USFM conversion, formatting changes, or other adaptations.
3. Avoid implying endorsement by either rights holder.
4. Apply CC BY-SA 4.0 to distributed adaptations of the scripture text.

These requirements must be translated into the product's content attribution, licence display, transformation provenance, and release-compliance controls before any content distribution.
