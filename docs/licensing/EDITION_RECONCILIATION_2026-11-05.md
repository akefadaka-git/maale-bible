## 2. Material identifier and rights discrepancy

The eBible record identifies the full Bible as `MDYTWF` / `mdyeth` and states Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 (CC BY-SA 4.0).

The Digital Bible Society record identifies a 2008 Maale full-Protestant-canon publication as `MDYBSE`, shows license status `RESERVED`, and names Bible Society of Ethiopia as publisher. It describes the work as produced by The Word for the World International and Bible Society of Ethiopia.

The eBible copyright index also uses `MDYBSE` for a distinct 2007 Maale New Testament publication, which is not the 2008 full Bible. The identifiers must therefore not be treated as interchangeable without publisher confirmation.

The DBL's own description explains that its library manages licensing documents and access agreements between copyright holders and publishing partners. A RESERVED status is a material access/licensing signal in that system; it is not, by itself, proof that the separate eBible CC BY-SA notice is invalid. The discrepancy requires written reconciliation from the rights holders and, if appropriate, DBL.

## 3. Preliminary legal interpretation — not a final clearance

If the exact 2008 full-Bible text is validly and authoritatively offered under CC BY-SA 4.0, the license generally permits sharing and adaptations subject to attribution, indication of modifications, and ShareAlike for redistributed adaptations. The eBible page states corresponding conditions.

However, this project must not rely solely on a third-party metadata page to establish:
- that both named copyright holders authorized the license statement;
- that the exact USFM package is the same work covered by that notice;
- that the license applies to the current package revision and all included material;
- that DBL's RESERVED record is the same digital asset or a separately administered release;
- that no third-party fonts, illustrations, audio, paratext, or other embedded assets carry separate rights.

No conclusion that publication is cleared has been reached.
