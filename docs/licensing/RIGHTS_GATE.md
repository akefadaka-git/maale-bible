## 4. Mandatory licence compliance

Any future distribution of the text or adaptations must satisfy CC BY-SA 4.0. At minimum:

1. Preserve the attribution: **Maale Bible (ጌኤዦ ማፃኣፖ) © 2008 The Word for the World International and the Bible Society of Ethiopia.**
2. Link to https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/.
3. Identify modifications, including conversion, formatting, normalization, corrections or other adaptations.
4. Do not imply endorsement by either rights holder.
5. Distribute adaptations of scripture text under CC BY-SA 4.0.
6. Preserve notices in exports and sharing where scripture text is included.
7. Keep audio, artwork, fonts, recordings, logos, maps and third-party metadata on separate rights tracks unless separately covered by evidence.
8. Do not assume the scripture licence automatically relicenses the entire application or independent user content.

Implementation controls LIC-01 through LIC-10 are tracked in [Licence Compliance Controls](LICENCE_COMPLIANCE_CONTROLS.md). Those are application/release acceptance controls, not claims that the product has already implemented them.

## 6. Release rule

A content-bearing release requires separate Owner authorization for intake and release, a retained provenance record, passing source validation, implemented attribution/licence/modification/ShareAlike controls, separately cleared assets, independent review, and explicit release approval. A2 source-readiness closure alone is not a release approval.
