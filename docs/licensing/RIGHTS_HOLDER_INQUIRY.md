# Rights-holder inquiry — Maale Full Bible (2008)

**To:** The Word for the World International; Bible Society of Ethiopia  
**Subject:** Written confirmation of edition identity and digital publishing rights — Maale Full Bible (MDYTWF / mdyeth)

Dear Rights and Publishing Team,

I am developing Maale Bible (ጌኤዦ ማፃኣፖ), an offline-first digital Bible application intended to make the Maale-language Scriptures accessible to Maale readers.

I am seeking written confirmation concerning the 2008 Maale full-Bible edition identified by eBible.org as `MDYTWF` / `mdyeth`:

- Language: Maale / Male (`mdy`)
- Script: Ethiopic
- Scope: full Bible / full Protestant canon
- Copyright notice shown by eBible.org: © 2008 The Word for the World International and The Bible Society of Ethiopia
- Translation agency: The Word for the World
- Contributor: Bible Society of Ethiopia
- eBible record: https://ebible.org/Scriptures/details.php?id=mdyeth

The eBible record states that this full-Bible edition is offered under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 (CC BY-SA 4.0) and lists a USFM download named `mdyeth_usfm.zip`.

I have also found a Digital Bible Society catalogue record for a 2008 Maale full-Protestant-canon publication under identifier `MDYBSE`, with Bible Society of Ethiopia as publisher and a `RESERVED` license status:
https://arc.dbs.org/data/scripture/MDYBSE/index.html

Before obtaining or using any source files, could you please confirm in writing:

1. Are `MDYTWF` / `mdyeth` and the Digital Bible Society `MDYBSE` record the same 2008 full-Bible edition and text, or do they refer to different releases or publications?
2. Did the relevant rights holders authorize the CC BY-SA 4.0 notice for the exact 2008 full-Bible text currently listed at eBible.org?
3. Does that license cover the current `mdyeth_usfm.zip` package and all of its textual and supporting files?
4. Does the DBL `RESERVED` status restrict or otherwise affect use of the eBible-distributed package under the stated CC BY-SA 4.0 license?
5. May this exact edition be included in an Android application distributed publicly, including offline storage, display, search indexing, navigation, bookmarks, and user sharing or quotation, subject to the applicable CC BY-SA 4.0 conditions?
6. What exact attribution and modification notices must appear in the application and its documentation?
7. Are any embedded materials (fonts, illustrations, maps, introductions, footnotes, or other paratext) excluded from the stated text license?
8. Is a separate signed permission letter or Digital Bible Library license agreement required for this application?
9. Who is the authorized representative who can issue or confirm the applicable permission on behalf of each rights holder?

I will not download, modify, or publish the scripture package until these questions are resolved. I am happy to provide the repository and project details for your review.

Thank you for helping ensure that this project respects the translation, its contributors, and the rights holders.

Sincerely,
