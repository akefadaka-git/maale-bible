## Permission and scope

Written confirmation from The Word for the World International, signed by Dr. Tesfaye Yohannes, East African representative, is recorded in the Owner-approved source-rights evidence. The authorized edition is the 2008 full Bible MDYTWF/MDYETH. The separate 2007 MDYBSE New Testament is not the source for this project.

The Genesis learning package is an original compilation of study guides, quizzes, commentary notes and reference aids. It contains no raw Scripture text and no direct Scripture quotations. Scripture references are locators, not reproduced text.
